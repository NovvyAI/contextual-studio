# Full analysis output template

## 1. Executive product thesis

State in two or three sentences:

- what the player repeatedly does;
- why that action feels good;
- what makes this presentation marketable or distinctive.

## 2. Store facts

Summarize platform, developer, category, rating, review count, download range when available, monetization labels, age rating, update date, and locale. Include an “as of” date for volatile values.

## 3. Product structure

Describe:

- atomic input and move rules;
- win and failure states;
- immediate feedback;
- level loop;
- meta and narrative loop;
- boosters, economy, ads, and purchases;
- confirmed versus unknown features.

## 4. Core selling points

Rank three to six selling points. For each, include:

- player-facing promise;
- supporting evidence;
- why it may convert;
- limitation or similarity to competitors.

## 5. Store creative analysis

Decode icon, screenshot sequence, trailer, copy, palette, UI, characters, transformations, and calls to action. Explain which assets sell core gameplay versus wrapper content.

## 6. Review and expectation signals

Summarize recurring praise, friction, product changes, and marketing mismatch. Attach dates to time-sensitive review observations.

## 7. Acquisition creative

When requested, separate:

- verified current or historical ads;
- store-derived creative patterns;
- inferred opportunities.

For each promising direction, provide hook, demonstrated mechanic, payoff, fidelity risk, and best audience emotion.

## 8. Strategic conclusion

State:

- the single strongest acquisition promise;
- the mechanic that must appear to preserve product truth;
- the most useful emotional or visual wrapper;
- the largest expectation or conversion risk;
- two or three testable creative territories.

## 9. Evidence notes

List inaccessible assets, market-specific uncertainty, missing gameplay evidence, incomplete ad-library coverage, and other limitations.
